# 1.9.9956 [RU](/ru/changelogs/9956)/[EN](/en/changelogs/9956)

A small patch with a few fixes.

## Fixes / improvements

* **[Window selection]** Fixed an issue where windows with `#1` and `#2` suffixes could get mixed up or switch on their own.
* **[Overlays]** Improved aspect-locked resizing: the overlay now correctly follows the side being dragged.
* **[UI]** Fixed restoring windows after minimizing them. Their contents should no longer disappear or shift.
