# Alpha - 1.9.9980 [RU](/ru/changelogs/9980)/[EN](/en/changelogs/9980)

Alpha 1.9.9980 lets you sign in to EyeAuras with Google, Twitch, or Discord.
Link a service to your account from [Profile](https://eyeauras.net/my), then sign in without your EyeAuras password. If you’re already signed in to the service in your browser, you may not need to enter your details again.
If you don’t have an EyeAuras account yet, your first service sign-in creates one automatically — no separate registration is needed.
If you already have licenses or history, sign in to your existing account first and link the service to avoid creating a new one.
[Read more about signing in with Google, Twitch, and Discord](/features/external-providers)

## Fixes / improvements

* **[Browser sign-in]** If sign-in is interrupted, reopen the window to continue or cancel it. Error messages are clearer, and a successful sign-in is saved on your computer.
* **[Interface]** Google, Twitch, and Discord buttons now have recognizable full-color icons and clear labels.
* **[Profile]** Know your current EyeAuras password? Open [Profile](https://eyeauras.net/my) and enter your current and new passwords. Google, Twitch, or Discord confirmation is not required. You’ll need to sign in again afterward.
* **[Older clients]** To create your first password or reset a forgotten one, sign in again through one of the currently available services already linked to your account. Then select `Create` or `Reset`. EyeAuras will show your username and new password after creating or resetting it. You won’t be able to view the password again later, so save both immediately.
* **[Username]** You can’t change your username yet. We plan to revisit this after sign-in and password recovery are stable. There is no timeline yet.
